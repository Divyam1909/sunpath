# Assumptions

Anything not stated in the client brief is listed here. Code comments and the TDD refer to these IDs. Values marked **editable** live in the admin Settings or rule tables, so changing them needs no deploy.

| ID | Assumption | Why | Where used |
|---|---|---|---|
| A01 | Sizes round to the nearest 0.5 kW, with a 1 kW minimum. **Editable.** | Reproduces the sample rows (2.91 → 3 kW; 0.73 → 1 kW) without oversizing, and 1 kW is the smallest common residential system. | `domain/sizing.ts` |
| A02 | Quote options sit 1 kW either side of the recommendation. **Editable.** | The brief says "one step either side". A 0.5 kW gap is too small to be a real choice for a homeowner. | `domain/quote.ts` |
| A03 | A system needs 10 m² of usable roof per kW. **Editable.** | Common industry rule of thumb for flat-mounted panels. The surveyor replaces it with a measured area. | `domain/sizing.ts` |
| A04 | System size cannot exceed sanctioned load. If load is unknown, no load cap applies. **Editable by removing the value.** | Net-metering applications are normally limited by sanctioned load. Load enhancement is a sales conversation, not an automatic step. | `domain/sizing.ts` |
| A05 | Prices: up to 2 kW ₹70,000/kW; up to 3 kW ₹65,000/kW; up to 5 kW ₹60,000/kW; up to 10 kW ₹55,000/kW; above ₹52,000/kW. The whole system is charged at its band's rate. **Editable.** | The brief gives no prices. These are placeholders to be replaced by Company A's real rate card before launch. | Seed, `domain/quote.ts` |
| A06 | Net metering: each generated unit offsets one consumed unit at the full tariff, up to yearly consumption. Surplus units earn ₹3 per unit. **Editable.** | The brief leaves crediting open. This stops an oversized system from looking better than it is. | `domain/quote.ts` |
| A07 | Tariff comes from the rate printed on the bill, else bill amount ÷ units, else ₹9 per unit. **Editable.** | Bill amount includes fixed charges, so ÷ units slightly overstates the rate. Sales can correct it in the quote builder. | `domain/quote.ts` |
| A08 | 25-year savings = yearly savings × 25 − net cost, with no tariff rise and no panel degradation. | Rising tariffs and panel ageing work in opposite directions. A flat model is simple to explain and does not oversell. | `domain/quote.ts` |
| A09 | Score points out of 100: owner 25; bill ≥ ₹2,000 30, or ₹1,000–1,999 10; roof fits need 20, or fits part 10; inside Vidarbha 15; clear bill read 10. High ≥ 90, Medium ≥ 50. **Thresholds editable.** | Reproduces the sample rows, and each point value can be explained to the client in one sentence. | `domain/score.ts` |
| A10 | Bills under ₹1,000 a month are always Low. **Editable.** | The client names "people with ₹600 bills" as the problem. ₹1,000 sits above the sample's ₹650. | `domain/score.ts` |
| A11 | Tenants are always Low and get no size until the owner is involved. | Sample row LD-30902 shows "-" for size and Low for a tenant. | `domain/score.ts` |
| A12 | Vidarbha means pincodes 440000–445999. | These postal prefixes cover Nagpur, Amravati and the surrounding districts. Needs a check against the client's actual service area. | `domain/pincode.ts` |
| A13 | A bill field under 75% confidence is highlighted for checking. **Editable.** | Model confidence is uncalibrated, so this is a starting point. Stored AI-vs-confirmed values allow tuning it later. | `domain/bill.ts` |
| A14 | Homeowners need no login for the form, quote or status page: unguessable links carry them. They can set a password on the result page to get a "My solar" page. | A password in a lead form would cut completion. The HOMEOWNER role still exists and is checked for accounts. | Auth, `/my` |
| A15 | Stage SLAs in days: Advance 3, Documents 7, Scheduled 14, Installed 5, Net-metering applied 21, Net meter installed 7, Subsidy applied 30. **Editable.** | The brief says "stuck" but gives no timings. | `domain/project.ts` |
| A16 | Required documents per stage are as listed in `REQUIRED_DOCS`, and a project cannot leave a stage until its documents are in. | The brief asks for a checklist per stage but does not list documents. | `domain/project.ts` |
| A17 | The advance is 20% of net cost. **Editable.** | The brief does not state it. Payment is mocked. | `server/quotes.ts` |
| A18 | Staff screens are English only; homeowner screens, the quote PDF and customer messages are English, Marathi and Hindi. | The brief allows this. Staff read English in the existing Excel workflow. | i18n |
| A19 | Marathi and Hindi copy is machine-drafted and needs a native review before launch. | No translator in a zero-cost build. | `messages/*.json` |
| A20 | The subsidy table is residential only. Bills on commercial tariffs (LT II and above) get the COMMERCIAL segment, which has no subsidy rule, so subsidy is ₹0. Sales can override the segment. | The brief's scheme is "central residential", yet the client also serves small businesses. | `domain/bill.ts` |
| A21 | Retention: lead data and bills 24 months after last activity for unconverted leads; project records 8 years for tax; rejected bill photos 90 days. | Placeholder for legal review. See the TDD. | TDD |
| A22 | The demo runs in Vercel `iad1` next to Neon `us-east-1` because that is what the free provisioning gave. Production should move to `ap-south-1`/`bom1`. | Latency and data-location concerns for a real launch. | `vercel.json` |
| A23 | Survey shading reduces usable roof: partial 20%, heavy 50%. It does not change generation. | The brief fixes generation to one formula. Shading therefore affects how much roof is usable. | Quote builder |
| A24 | Monthly units for sizing are the average of the months on the bill. With one month and no history, that month is scaled by the season (A36). | The sample column is "Avg Units/Month", and one month can mislead. | `domain/usage.ts` |
| A25 | With no bill read, units are estimated as monthly bill ÷ ₹9. | The lead must still be scored when AI is down. It earns no confidence points. | `server/leads.ts` |
| A26 | "Sunpath" is the working product name, and the customer-facing brand shows "Company A Solar". | The brief names no product. | UI |
| A27 | Every outbound update is a mock WhatsApp row in `MessageOutbox`. | The brief says to mock messages. | `server/outbox.ts` |
| A28 | "Same day" is measured on the Indian calendar day. | A UTC day boundary would misreport evening surveys. | `domain/format.ts` |
| A29 | Seeded records use fixed, readable link tokens (for example `demo-quote-ld-31007`) so the README can link straight to them. Real records always get 192 random bits. | A reviewer needs one-click links. Only fictional demo data has guessable links. | `prisma/seed.ts` |
| A30 | The default model is `gemini-3.5-flash`, then `gemini-3.5-flash-lite` when the first is over its limit. Both can be changed by env var. | The brief's `gemini-2.5-flash` returns 404 for new keys. The free tier allowed only 20 requests a day on the first model, which a live demo can use up. | `server/bill-reader` |
| A31 | If Gemini fails on a known sample bill, the mock reader answers and the reading records `mock`. Unknown bills go to manual entry. | Keeps the demo working through a free-tier outage without inventing values for a real bill. | `server/bill-reader` |
| A32 | A quote can only be built once a survey is recorded. | The brief says the quote follows the survey. | Lead detail |
| A33 | A deletion request is handled by an admin using "Erase customer data", which removes the lead, files, quotes, project and messages. | The consent notice promises deletion, so there has to be a way to do it. | `server/leads.ts` |
| A34 | Unknown quote and status links show the not-found page with `noindex`, over a 200 status, because the loading skeleton has already streamed. | Keeping skeletons on navigation mattered more than the status code. No data is exposed either way. | `app/[locale]/loading.tsx` |
| A35 | Monthly solar output follows PVGIS 5.3 for a south-facing roof in Nagpur (ERA5, 2005 to 2023), used as shares of the year. The brief's 1,400 units per kW still sets the yearly total. | The brief gives a yearly figure, but monsoon months make about half of March. PVGIS's own total for Nagpur is about 1,497. | `domain/settings.ts` |
| A36 | With no history, a month is scaled by an assumed use pattern: May 1.33 times the average, December 0.8. A bill 20% or more from the average is flagged. | Summer cooling drives the peak. The shape is an estimate, checked only against two real homes. Replace it with confirmed bills once there are enough. | `domain/usage.ts` |
| A37 | The bill view nets export against import each month at full slab rates, banks spare units, and pays the balance on 31 March at the ₹3 surplus rate. | This follows how net-metered MSEDCL bills show import, export and bank. Self-use within the month is treated the same as export. | `domain/projection.ts` |
| A38 | Quotes keep the brief's one-rate formula, so the sample rows still reproduce, and the slab view sits beside it as a second figure. | The brief fixes the formula. Slab savings are usually higher, so the quote's figure is the cautious one. | `components/quote` |
| A39 | "Solar may not be worth it" shows when payback passes 12 years. | Editable. With today's subsidy even an 85-unit home pays back in about 5 years, so this rarely shows. The real data disproved the idea that small users are poor buyers. | `domain/projection.ts` |
| A40 | Tariffs are MSEDCL LT-I(B) from MERC Case 75 of 2025. FAC is copied from the October 2026 bill and changes monthly. The ₹10 municipal adder and the daytime rebate are left out of projections. | Both exclusions affect before and after alike, or depend on a smart meter. | `prisma/seed-rules.ts` |
| A41 | Blockers: a name that does not match the applicant, arrears over ₹500, a load below the wanted size, a non-home tariff, a tenant, existing solar export, a meter not marked normal. | Drawn from common scheme and net-meter requirements. Confirm each with MSEDCL and the scheme portal before launch. They inform people and never change the score. | `domain/blockers.ts` |
| A42 | A bill's total is reduced by its arrears before working out a rate per unit or scoring the bill size. | Old dues say nothing about this month's electricity. A real bill with ₹2,986 of arrears otherwise looked like ₹20 a unit. | `domain/bill.ts` |
| A43 | A bill after solar is on track at 90% of the promised saving, needs watching from 75%, and is behind below that. | Editable. Bills vary with weather and use, so a small shortfall is not a fault. Compared with the same calendar month in the frozen quote. | `domain/promise.ts` |
| A44 | Forecasts use the median and 80th percentile of finished projects, per stage, once a stage has 3 of them. Below that they use the stage limit. | Few samples prove little. The demo's history is seed data, so its dates show the method, not a real result. | `domain/forecast.ts` |
| A45 | The roof map uses Esri World Imagery and OpenStreetMap search with no key. | Free for a demo. Check both providers' terms before commercial use. The outline is plan area, so a sloped roof reads smaller, and a surveyor still measures. | `components/public/roof-map.tsx` |
| A46 | The photo check warns under 450 px on the short side, under brightness 70, or under sharpness 20. It never blocks. | Set from test images: Gemini still read bills at sharpness 35 and brightness 114, so warnings must not be strict. | `lib/photo-check.ts` |
| A47 | Lookalike bills copy the layout of published bills with fictional details, tariff-correct numbers, a SAMPLE stamp, and no logo, QR code or barcode. | They give many cases with exact answers, without using anyone's data. They are reported apart from real bills. | `scripts/lookalike-bills.ts` |
